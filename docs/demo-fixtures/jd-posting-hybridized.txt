Senior Engineering Manager, ML Platform & Trust Infrastructure
Vireo Systems
Location: Remote - USA; San Francisco, CA; Seattle, WA (Hybrid, 3 days/week in a hub)
Employment Type: Full time
Compensation: $260,000 - $360,000 OTE, plus equity

About the Team
The ML Platform team builds the training pipelines, feature infrastructure, and
evaluation systems behind every risk score Vireo returns, across hundreds of
customers and billions of events a year. We sit inside Vireo's Data & ML
organization, alongside teams building the agentic AI layer for Vireo's
internal finance and operations tooling -- the same evaluation and governance
discipline that keeps a fraud score trustworthy also has to keep an
AI-generated financial answer trustworthy, and this role owns both.

What We're Looking For
We're hiring a Senior Engineering Manager to lead this team: an inspiring,
technical manager who brings focus to what matters now without losing sight
of the long term. You're comfortable being the founding engineering leader
for a build-from-scratch mandate as well as the steady hand raising the bar
on a mature production system. You value collaboration and transparency,
operate with a get-stuff-done mindset, and have the technical depth to spot
the manual, brittle, or duplicated work quietly slowing the team down. You
build a culture of mentorship, give regular and constructive feedback, set
clear goals, and grow your team by hiring effectively.

Projects You Might Lead

* Launch a unified model evaluation framework that gives Data Science and ML
  Engineering fast, trustworthy, apples-to-apples comparisons before a model
  or an AI agent ever reaches production.
* Evolve core feature infrastructure -- including a new global feature store
  -- to improve accuracy and unlock faster experimentation.
* Own the AI/LLM roadmap for an internal agentic tooling platform: RAG
  pipelines, prompt and model versioning, and a governed, semantic data
  layer, built from an early stage rather than inherited.
* Enforce governance by design -- role-based access control at the data
  model level, not the application layer, so speed and automation never
  come at the cost of data governance.
* Build automation that detects an active fraud attack, adjusts score
  calibration in real time, and cleanly reverts once it subsides.
* Introduce agentic, AI-assisted tooling into customer investigations,
  automating repetitive data pulls and validation so analysts spend their
  time on judgment calls, not manual digging.

What You'll Do

* Lead and grow the team: own the roadmap, execution, and quality of the
  systems that train, evaluate, and serve Vireo's ML models and AI agents in
  production, leading a team of ML platform engineers and data scientists.
* Stay technical: review designs, unblock engineers on hard problems, and
  make credible calls on architecture and trade-offs; step in on
  implementation when it matters.
* Be the stakeholder-facing technical lead: act as the primary engineering
  liaison to business partners and leadership, translating ambiguous
  requirements into a scoped, buildable roadmap and representing
  engineering trade-offs credibly to technical and non-technical audiences.
* Raise the production engineering bar: models and agents ship as
  dependable software -- clear APIs, tests, CI/CD, observability, on-call
  ownership, and defined reliability targets.
* Build the ML and agent operating model: shared approaches to evaluation,
  golden sets, training-data provenance, model and prompt versioning,
  monitoring, retraining, latency, and cost. Replace artisanal evaluation
  with repeatable systems.
* Reduce technical debt: drive a sustained, measurable reduction in
  technical debt across the platform, balancing new feature delivery with
  the health of existing systems.
* Partner cross-functionally: align platform investments with business
  priorities alongside Data Science, Core Infrastructure, Product, Security,
  and Customer Success.
* Model AI-native leadership: use AI to increase the team's speed and
  ambition while keeping ownership of every output -- as generated code
  gets cheaper, raise the bar on judgment, verification, and system design.

GCP, AWS, Spark, Kafka, Kubernetes, Docker, Databricks, Python, and a modern
